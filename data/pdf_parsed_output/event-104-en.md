First municipal evaluation in the new SILEX cycle to test searches and procedure records with Santa Marina staff, record usability issues and prioritize corrections before pre-production.

Javier Ortega: Six citizen-service staff and two unit managers are participating. We will evaluate sixteen representative tasks.
Luis Noguera: The tasks cover resident registration, licences, grants and citizen requests. We will also test filters by unit and deadline.
Alba Prieto: Fourteen of the sixteen tasks were completed without assistance. The two failures involved finding a procedure by an abbreviated name and understanding a date calculated after a suspension.
Mireia Soler: The calculated date is correct, but the interface shows only the result. It must explain the suspended period and resumption date.
Nuria Castaño: Multi-filter searches now respond in under two seconds in this environment. The abbreviated-name problem can be resolved with controlled synonyms.
Luis Noguera: Staff also ask for a clearer distinction between the responsible unit and the service point, because they are sometimes different.
Clara Ibáñez: Does any of this block pre-production on 21 September?
Javier Ortega: No, provided that the three corrections are delivered by 25 August: deadline explanation, synonyms and visual separation of units.
Alba Prieto: Average satisfaction was 4.1 out of 5. Users particularly valued the legal source and update date.
Luis Noguera: The municipality agrees to continue preparation, subject to a second evaluation in September.
Javier Ortega: Correction and a second round are approved.
