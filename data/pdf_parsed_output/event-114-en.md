Third SILEX municipal evaluation, held at the end of pilot week, to review real use, incidents, criteria compliance and acceptance of the system by Santa Marina.

Javier Ortega: The pilot ran from 9 through 13 November with eight citizen-service staff and four municipal units. We will review results and incidents.
Luis Noguera: There were 386 queries and 74 guided-processing cases. No service interruptions occurred.
Nuria Castaño: Availability was 99.96 percent. The 95th percentile remained at 1.8 seconds, and the maximum recorded was 3.4 during a scheduled import.
Alba Prieto: Functional coverage was 100 percent for all 62 procedures. Three queries were unresolved because local expressions were missing from the synonym catalogue.
Mireia Soler: We will add them as alternative terms without modifying identifiers or the model.
Sergio Vidal: Every access, response and version was recorded. No divergence was detected between pilot data and the approved copy.
Clara Ibáñez: Was there any critical incident or failure to meet the criteria?
Luis Noguera: No. There were two minor training issues and one suggestion to export a procedure record as PDF.
Javier Ortega: The criteria were at least 95 percent coverage, zero critical errors and response below two seconds for the agreed queries. All three are met.
Luis Noguera: Santa Marina accepts the system and recommends moving to controlled operation for three months.
Javier Ortega: The pilot is approved. Minor improvements will be included in the follow-up plan without blocking closure.
