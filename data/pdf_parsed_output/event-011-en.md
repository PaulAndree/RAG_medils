Third SILEX requirements follow-up meeting to review legal authorization for the resident-registration dataset, close the four incomplete procedures and update dependencies in the mapping catalogue.

Javier Ortega: The objective is to determine whether we can close the requirements that blocked the original pilot date.
Luis Noguera: The legal office has authorized reuse of the resident-registration dataset for the pilot, provided that we use the anonymized export and do not publish individual records.
Sergio Vidal: That condition is compatible with the planned environment. We will retain only temporary technical identifiers and aggregated metrics.
Alba Prieto: We have also received the four corrected procedures. They now include the responsible unit, deadline, legal basis and required documentation.
Mireia Soler: Two modeling decisions remain: how to represent suspension of deadlines and how to link a municipal unit whose name changes during the year.
Javier Ortega: Those decisions will be resolved at the next workshop. Are any functional requirements still open?
Alba Prieto: We only need to confirm the pilot acceptance criteria: at least 95 percent coverage, no critical errors and API response below two seconds for the agreed queries.
Luis Noguera: The municipality accepts those three criteria.
Javier Ortega: Excellent. Alba will close the requirements catalogue, Sergio will add the anonymization condition, and Mireia will prepare the two outstanding decisions for the 27 May workshop.
