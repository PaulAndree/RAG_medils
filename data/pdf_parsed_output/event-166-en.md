Third ARIADNA retrieval comparison to evaluate v2.2-rc1 over a reserved maintenance set, measure abstention, unnecessary rejection and clarification quality without reusing the official v2.1 test set.

Andrés Beltrán: The reserved maintenance set contains 300 questions and was not used to select thresholds or templates.
Sergio Vidal: It includes 180 answerable and 120 unanswerable questions. Twenty-four present conflicting current sources to test the proposed new reason.
Tomás Leal: v2.2-rc1 achieves 85.1 percent correct abstention and 8.4 percent unnecessary abstention. Accuracy over answerable questions is 69.3 percent.
Lucía Navarro: How do the five reasons perform?
Tomás Leal: no-evidence reaches 88 percent, out-of-validity 91 percent, language-unavailable 87 percent, underspecified-scope 79 percent and conflicting-current-evidence 75 percent.
Pablo Herrero: The last reason is harder because the system finds evidence but must recognize that it cannot resolve the contradiction.
Nuria Castaño: In those cases, the interface shows both sources, their dates and a recommendation for human review. It does not generate a synthesis as though one answer existed.
Andrés Beltrán: What is the quality of the clarifications?
Tomás Leal: Eighty-four percent add one dimension and preserve intent. The remainder combine date and unit or formulate an overly general clarification.
