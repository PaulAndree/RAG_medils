FINAL LEGAL ASSESSMENT FOR REUSE OF MUNICIPAL DATA IN SILEX V1.0

1. Status

This assessment supersedes preliminary assessment v0.8. It incorporates responses from the Santa Marina City Council, licence conditions for the exports, and decisions concerning demonstration, transfer, and retention.

The main conclusion is that SILEX may publish procedure metadata and use synthetic records or authorized samples in internal tests. Personal content from the municipal register is not part of the open catalogue or general transfer package.

2. Information categories

Procedure metadata, case data, personal data from the register, technical identifiers, audit records, and project documentation are distinguished. Every category has a purpose, access level, retention period, and permitted products.

Titles, descriptions, units, channels, deadlines, and legal bases may be added to the catalogue where their source and conditions permit. Personal contact names are excluded unless expressly required.

3. Municipal register

The registration-certificate procedure may be described without copying individual records. Any connection test uses synthetic data or a minimum sample in a restricted environment. Authorization for an internal test is not a redistribution licence.

4. Publication

Release requires a licence, provenance, field review, and absence of unnecessary personal data. A record with a pending licence remains outside the package. The exclusion list retains the reason so counts are verifiable.
