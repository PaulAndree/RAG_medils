The sample received from SolCentro contains readings every 15 minutes rather than hourly data as assumed in the original plan. Each meter generates 96 observations per day instead of 24. The change increases the expected volume by approximately four times and affects the connector schema, continuity validation, and aggregation calculations.

Available fields are imported energy, exported energy, average power, and reading quality. The timestamp is in local time and includes the UTC offset, so the offset must not be removed during ingestion. Specific daylight-saving-time tests are required.

It has not been decided whether raw data will be shared outside SolCentro. Until the privacy assessment is complete, the connector must retain the original granularity at the source and generate aggregated views for other participants.
