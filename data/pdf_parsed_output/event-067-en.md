Third bilingual annotation workshop. The session records the controlled reopening of the corpus because several travel questions rely on rules superseded after the April freeze.

Andrés Beltrán: The 19 June replication was consistent, but Clara identified a document issue that must be resolved before the report is closed.
Clara Ibáñez: The university published new travel rules on 15 May. They change accommodation limits, prior authorization and the deadline for submitting expenses. Corpus v2.0 contains the previous rules as the current document.
Rosa Mena: In addition, the English version of the new rules was published on 12 June. Between those dates, the current evidence differed by language.
Tomás Leal: The test contains eighteen travel questions. Eleven ask about current rules and would be outdated; seven are historical and remain valid if their dates are preserved.
Sergio Vidal: This is not evaluation leakage. It is subsequent obsolescence after the freeze, but it affects the benchmark's temporal validity.
Lucía Navarro: We must replace the rules, update version relations and review bilingual pairs while respecting the different publication dates.
Pablo Herrero: I propose retaining the historical questions, replacing the eleven current questions and adding examples that require distinguishing 15 May from 12 June.
Andrés Beltrán: Does reopening invalidate all corrected metrics?
Sergio Vidal: No. We can calculate the impact on the affected subset and rerun the full evaluation after the new freeze. The previous metrics still describe v2.0, but they must not be presented as final results for the updated corpus.
Andrés Beltrán: A reopening limited to travel rules and related questions is approved. The rest of the corpus remains locked. The new version will be v2.1 and will freeze on 17 July after audit.
