Second VERDANT pilot-closure session to close the summer expansion phase, consolidate lessons learned and transfer monitoring of six facilities to the autumn evaluation.

Andrés Beltrán: This session closes the summer phase, not the entire pilot. Governance authorized continuation through 30 November.
Inés Ferrer: Between 10 August and 30 September, we processed 44,208 hourly records and five local runs. Scientific indicators meet the planned objectives.
Sergio Vidal: Ninety requests were recorded during the phase: 84 approved and six rejected. There were no granularity breaches or redistribution incidents.
Nuria Castaño: Availability was 99.9 percent. Two retention alerts and one synchronization error were corrected without loss of authorized data.
Carmen Rey: The main operational cost is manual review of local results. For 2027, checks on group size and permitted ranges should be automated.
Héctor Molina: Dynamic suppression and local processing have proved viable alternatives to detailed-data export. The prohibition on raw 15-minute series should remain.
Bruno Silva: The international partner confirms that hourly data is sufficient for the autumn evaluation.
Andrés Beltrán: Approved lessons: monitoring from the beginning, UTC validation, automatic rotation and differentiation between suppression and failure.
Sergio Vidal: The autumn phase will use the same policy v1.0 and produce a final report comparing utility, risk and operational load.
Andrés Beltrán: The summer phase closes successfully. The pilot continues through November under the current controls.
