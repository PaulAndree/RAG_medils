Technical workshop to define the main classes and properties of the municipal-services ontology and review examples taken from the requirements catalogue.

Mireia Soler: I propose separating an administrative procedure from each of its specific calls or instances.
Alba Prieto: That separation works for grants, but we need to check how it applies to licences, which do not always have a call.
Yara Ben Salem: We could use a general procedure class with specializations for continuous procedures and procedures subject to a call.
Javier Ortega: It is important that the model does not depend solely on the current organization of Santa Marina City Council. It should be reusable by other municipalities.
Sergio Vidal: In the source data, the responsible unit is sometimes represented as free text and sometimes through an internal code.
Mireia Soler: We will therefore need a separate administrative-unit entity and a mapping catalogue for local codes.
Alba Prieto: We must also represent whether administrative silence has a positive, negative or non-applicable outcome.
Javier Ortega: Agreed. Mireia will prepare a first class diagram. Alba will add examples from the four priority areas, and Yara will review the Spanish and English labels.
