First design session in the new LUMEN demonstrator cycle to integrate rights states, uncertain dates, archival roles and objects from the second ingestion into a coherent interface.

Lucía Navarro: The objective is to design a view that combines metadata, reproduction, rights, dates and entities without confusing users.
Nuria Castaño: The record can have four blocks: identification, description, relationships and conditions of use. The reproduction will be shown only when policy permits it.
Eva Calderón: The metadata-only state must be visible. We do not want the absence of an image to look like a technical error.
Mateo Ruiz: For uncertain dates, I propose showing the original expression first and then the normalized interpretation, for example 'circa 1910' and 'estimated interval 1908–1912'.
Yara Ben Salem: Labels must retain the same function in both languages. We must not translate productor del fondo as creator without the records creator clarification.
Diego Ramos: Entity links will distinguish confirmed, candidate and unresolved. Users will be able to view the evidence supporting a suggestion.
Nuria Castaño: I also need to decide how to present permissions that expire.
Eva Calderón: Display the licence and expiry date, but the system must automatically withdraw the reproduction when permission is no longer valid.
Lucía Navarro: We approve the four-block structure, explicit explanation of missing images, dual date representation and the three linking states.
Mateo Ruiz: I will prepare twenty example records for the 6 August evaluation.
