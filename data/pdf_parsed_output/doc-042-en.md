ARIADNA COMPLEX-CASE ADJUDICATION REGISTER V1.0

Purpose

This register documents decisions on questions whose category, answer, date, difficulty, or evidence produced disagreement. Every case retains the initial labels, arguments, sources, final decision, and artifacts requiring updates.

Procedure

Two people review the case before the session. Adjudication consults the complete source rather than snippets alone. The person proposing the question may explain the intent but does not decide alone. The decision applies to the ES/EN pair.

Session decisions

Twelve cases were resolved. Four questions became temporal because the answer depended on a version valid on a specific date. Three became multi-hop because they required a relation among an event, task, and owner. Two became unanswerable because no explicit final date existed. Three were split or simplified because they contained two separate questions.

Examples

For a visit extension, a note explains the rationale, while a later email confirms the date. The answer must distinguish proposal from approval. For a pilot date, a provisional calendar cannot be treated as final without formal confirmation.

Traceability

Every decision has an identifier, date, participants, initial category, final category, explanation, sources, and update owner. Reopened cases retain history.

Adjudication rule 1

Where two sources appear contradictory, the session checks validity, purpose, and status. A proposal and an approval are not contradictory where they represent different phases. The decision must explain which source answers the question.

Adjudication rule 2

A question containing two independent requests is divided where each part can be evaluated separately. It is not retained as one merely to increase difficulty. New pairs receive identifiers and relations to the original case.
