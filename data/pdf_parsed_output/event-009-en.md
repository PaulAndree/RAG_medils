Second SILEX pilot steering committee. The committee reviews the missed mapping-stabilization milestone and concludes that the original week beginning 21 September 2026 is no longer viable. The week beginning 9 November 2026 is proposed provisionally, subject to confirmation.

Javier Ortega: At the previous committee meeting, we retained the week beginning 21 September as the baseline date, conditional on stabilizing the mappings before 30 April.
Sergio Vidal: After validating the new export, nine mappings still contain errors. Four affect mandatory fields, and three depend on the resident-registration dataset licence.
Alba Prieto: In addition, two procedures use unit codes that are not included in the municipal catalogue we received.
Luis Noguera: We can correct the codes during the first week of May. Legal authorization for the registration dataset will not arrive before 15 May.
Mireia Soler: Even if everything arrives on the 15th, we will need at least four weeks to complete the rules, repeat validation and prepare the environment.
Clara Ibáñez: With that schedule, can we retain September?
Javier Ortega: Not with a reasonable margin. I propose removing the week beginning 21 September as the operational date and provisionally working toward the week beginning 9 November.
Luis Noguera: The municipality can reserve that week, but it needs confirmation before 30 June.
Clara Ibáñez: I will record 21 September as the discarded original date and 9 November as the provisional date. The final date will be approved once validation is complete and the municipality confirms availability.
Javier Ortega: Agreed. We will also update the risk plan and communicate the change through a formal email.
