Two aggregation levels were compared for data leaving the cooperative. Hourly aggregation preserves enough detail to evaluate connector operation and detect incidents. Daily aggregation reduces the risk of identifying consumption habits further, but limits technical analysis.

Working proposal:
- For the technical pilot, export hourly sums of imported and exported energy and average power.
- Do not export intervals to which fewer than three meters contribute.
- For non-operational external analysis, provide daily aggregates only.
- Keep 15-minute readings inside SolCentro's infrastructure.

The recommendation still requires confirmation by the ethics committee. It must not be implemented as final policy. Sergio will prepare a test using seven days of data, and Inés will measure the loss of utility relative to the original data. Every view must record its aggregation rule and generation date.
