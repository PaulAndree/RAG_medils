ARIADNA USER EVALUATION AND CONSENT PROTOCOL V1.0

1. Purpose

This protocol defines user evaluation of the ARIADNA system with staff from the Research Office at Sierra Centro Public University. It covers objectives, participants, recruitment, consent, tasks, metrics, collected data, security, retention, analysis, incidents, and closure.

The study evaluates whether people can locate answers, interpret citations, distinguish current versions, understand abstention, and use language switching. It does not assess individual job performance or make decisions about participants.

2. Background

A preliminary test with eight people produced thirty-seven comments. Three high-priority issues were identified: citation links did not always open the exact passage, answers did not clearly show when a policy was superseded, and the absence message did not distinguish missing evidence from retrieval failure.

Priority corrections must be verified before the main evaluation. History and export functions are treated as future improvements rather than entry conditions.

3. Participants

Recruitment targets twelve to sixteen Research Office staff with varied experience in projects, travel, policies, calls, and administration. Participation is voluntary. People whose direct relationship with the evaluation team could make refusal difficult are not recruited.

No sensitive categories are collected. Broad professional profile, approximate search-system experience, and starting language are recorded. Information is used only to interpret aggregated results.

4. Consent

The information sheet explains the purpose, activities, duration, risks, benefits, voluntariness, withdrawal, data, retention, and contact details. A person may stop the session or withdraw their data before analysis closes without giving a reason.

Ethics approval conditions the study on not recording complete text from free queries. Sessions use prepared tasks or interaction metrics. Any change to participants, purpose, or collected data requires an amendment.
