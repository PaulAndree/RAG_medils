The data dictionary covers 94 of the 108 fields included in the reusable export. Definitions are missing for six software fields, four affiliation-history fields, and four quality indicators generated during migration.

Software fields must state whether a URL refers to the repository, documentation, package, or project page. For affiliations, effective date, registration date, and inferred date must be distinguished. Quality indicators will not be part of the public corpus but must be documented to explain why particular records were excluded or sent for review.

Sergio will complete definitions using code, exports, and archived documentation. Elena will review fields related to projects and publications. No field will be marked mandatory until its actual coverage has been checked. The final dictionary will include type, cardinality, example, provenance, restrictions, and migration notes.
