First dedicated ARIADNA review of unanswerable questions to classify cases affected by temporal validity, missing translations, insufficient scope and genuine lack of evidence during v2.1 reconstruction.

Andrés Beltrán: The limited corpus reopening caused by the travel rules showed that we need to distinguish more clearly why a question cannot be answered.
Lucía Navarro: I reviewed 74 cases marked unanswerable. Twenty-two lack evidence in the corpus, eighteen ask about a date outside the validity interval, fourteen request a language version that did not yet exist and twenty have ambiguous scope.
Rosa Mena: For travel, an English question before 12 June must not use the new English rules as evidence, even though the Spanish rules had been current since 15 May.
Tomás Leal: For retrieval, we should separate absence of a document from retrieval of a document that explicitly lacks the requested information.
Pablo Herrero: The tool can store a reasonCode with four values: no-evidence, out-of-validity, language-unavailable and underspecified-scope.
Sergio Vidal: The test set should preserve these proportions, but identifiers will remain hidden from the modeling team.
Lucía Navarro: How do we treat an ambiguous question that could be resolved through clarification?
Andrés Beltrán: Mark it underspecified-scope and record an answerable reformulation, but the original answer remains 'unanswerable'.
Tomás Leal: That will allow us to evaluate whether the system abstains and, separately, whether it proposes a useful clarification.
Andrés Beltrán: We approve the four reasons, linked reformulation and separation between abstention and user assistance. The 74 cases will be reannotated before the 17 July freeze.
