Five proposals were received: agents for knowledge management, multilingual RAG evaluation, verifiable data-space governance, semantic preservation of archives, and quality tools for knowledge graphs.

The preliminary assessment identifies multilingual RAG evaluation, verifiable governance, and quality tools as priorities. All three build on existing projects, have available data, and match identified funding calls. Agents require further risk analysis, and semantic preservation needs confirmation of an additional archive partner.

Every priority must present a scientific question, resources, data, partners, risks, and a funding opportunity. Elena will prepare the retreat discussion. The other proposals will not be discarded; they will remain exploratory lines with one concrete action to reduce their main uncertainty.
