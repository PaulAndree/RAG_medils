A total of 320 projects, 1,840 publications, 96 datasets, and 74 software records were selected for inclusion in ARIADNA's administrative corpus. All have stable identifiers, complete basic relations, and a licence compatible with internal benchmark use.

Fifteen historical cases will also be retained as controlled distractors: five resolved duplicate profiles, four affiliations with an old date, three corrected titles, and three obsolete project relations. Every distractor will be marked as a historical version or superseded record and will have a canonical source that allows the conflict to be resolved.

Internal metrics, audit records, personal email addresses, and the eight profiles whose identity remains unresolved will not be included. Sergio will generate the package, and Tomás will design control questions to verify that the system prioritizes current evidence without losing the ability to answer historical questions.
