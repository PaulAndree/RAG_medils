Five Santa Marina City Council staff members tested search and the procedure-detail view. Overall feedback was positive, but four priority improvements were identified. The unit filter uses internal codes that are not understandable and should also display the current name. Deadlines appear in a technical format and need a readable label. The interface does not distinguish clearly enough between missing information and data awaiting validation. Finally, changing language returns users to the home screen instead of preserving the open procedure.

Synonym search worked well for the Spanish terms for registration but failed for “terrace permit” versus “occupation of public space.” Alba will add equivalents to the vocabulary, and Nuria will preserve context when users switch languages.

Municipal staff will repeat the tasks after the corrections. The comments do not affect data integrity, but two problems are considered pilot blockers: deadline display and loss of context between languages.
