Second LUMEN terminology workshop to review twenty archival examples and establish a provisional distinction among author, material creator, audiovisual producer and records creator before updating the bilingual guide.

Lucía Navarro: We have reviewed the twenty examples. Eva, what are the main patterns?
Eva Calderón: In twelve cases, the entity described as producer is the organization that created or accumulated the fonds in the course of its functions. Five cases actually concern the intellectual author. The remaining three involve audiovisual production.
Mateo Ruiz: Guide version 1.0 does not distinguish those uses and groups them under creator.
Diego Ramos: That grouping explains several incorrect links. The system attributes individual documents to the institution responsible for the fonds.
Yara Ben Salem: I propose four separate roles: author for intellectual responsibility; material creator for physical creation when relevant; audiovisual producer for technical production; and records creator for the entity that creates or accumulates records through its functions.
Mireia Soler: We can retain contributor as the general relation, but queries and the interface must use the specific roles.
Eva Calderón: I agree, on one condition: records creator must be defined with archival examples and must not be automatically translated as the creator of every document.
Lucía Navarro: The proposal is approved provisionally. Yara will draft bilingual definitions, Mateo will add examples, and Diego will rerun the linking evaluation.
Diego Ramos: I also need to update training data that used creator as a single category.
Lucía Navarro: Do that, but version 1.0 of the guide will remain current until we validate the demonstrator. The revised version will be 1.1.
