Hello Lucía,

The proposed distinction is appropriate with two adjustments. In photographic collections, the studio may be the creator of the fonds, while the photographer is the author of the image. For digitization, the archive or supplier may be the producer of the digital copy, but that function must not be inherited by the original document.

We have marked three examples requiring correction and added one letter in which creator and author correspond to different entities. We recommend always preserving the function in the original archival description and recording any normalization as a separate decision.

With these changes, we approve the proposal for guide v1.1.
