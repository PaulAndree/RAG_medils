SILEX MUNICIPAL PILOT WORK PLAN V1.1

1. Status and relation to the previous version

Version 1.1 supersedes SILEX work plan v1.0. It retains the scientific objectives and overall architecture while updating the schedule, deployment sequence, municipal dependencies, and readiness criteria. The earlier version remains archived and should be consulted to reconstruct the decisions leading to the change.

The pilot originally planned for September is delayed by three dependencies: confirmation of pending fields in the municipal export, configuration of the pre-production network, and final review of API messages. The new operating window is set from 5 to 16 October 2026. The date is treated as approved following joint confirmation by NEXUS and the Santa Marina City Council.

2. Objective

SILEX demonstrates that a heterogeneous municipal catalogue can be transformed into an interoperable, bilingual, and verifiable semantic representation. The pilot covers the catalogue, ontology, mappings, API, deployment, training, evaluation with municipal staff, transfer, and closure.

Version 1.1 strengthens the distinction between proposal and approval. Dates, requirements, and exceptions must retain their status. A coordination conversation may produce a proposal, but the plan changes only where a formal recorded decision exists.

3. Principles

The project does not invent values to complete missing fields. Every transformation retains provenance. Spanish and English versions share identifiers and meaning. Validation runs before publication or deployment. Legal and organizational decisions take precedence over technical availability.

Data is classified as present, missing, non-applicable, excluded, or pending. This classification affects SHACL rules, the interface, reports, and metrics. An empty field without an explicit status is treated as a defect.
