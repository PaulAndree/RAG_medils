Third LUMEN terminology workshop to approve the final definitions of author, material creator, audiovisual producer and records creator, close bilingual guide 1.1 and declare guide 1.0 superseded.

Lucía Navarro: We will review the final text of guide 1.1 and the observations from the demonstrator test.
Yara Ben Salem: The bilingual definitions distinguish four roles. Author corresponds to intellectual responsibility; material creator to relevant physical creation; audiovisual producer to technical production; and records creator to the entity that creates or accumulates the fonds through its functions.
Eva Calderón: The Spanish version uses autor, creador material, productor audiovisual and productor del fondo. Each term includes examples and usage notes.
Mateo Ruiz: We have also added a warning: productor del fondo must not be interpreted as the author of every document contained in the fonds.
Diego Ramos: The new classification raised linking precision to 92 percent and eliminated most incorrect attributions.
Mireia Soler: The model retains contributor as the upper-level relation, but it requires one of the specific roles whenever the source provides sufficient evidence.
