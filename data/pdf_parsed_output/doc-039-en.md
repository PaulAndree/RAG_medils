VERDANT DATA GOVERNANCE AND ACCESS POLICY V1.0

Purpose

This policy defines who may discover, request, generate, receive, and retain VERDANT products. It applies to SolCentro, NEXUS, and Instituto Técnico do Norte during the pilot and transfer.

Principles

The source retains authority over data. Minimization, specific purpose, least privilege, traceability, expiry, and revocation apply. Fifteen-minute data remains at SolCentro. External products are hourly or daily and require at least three meters.

Roles

The data owner approves purpose and products. The data steward maintains the catalogue, quality, and policies. The connector operator administers the service. The consumer requests products. The security reviewer verifies controls. The auditor reads evidence without changing data.

Decisions

Every product has an identifier, purpose, schema, frequency, minimum group, owner, policy, version, and review date. An exception has a scope and expiry. Absence of a response is not interpreted as approval.

Access

Policy evaluates identity, role, product, operation, purpose, period, and environment. A valid credential is insufficient by itself. The administration endpoint is limited to authorized networks.

Retention

Products have a retention period. Temporary copies are deleted, and deletion is verified. Audit records are retained for thirty days and contain no complete bodies.

Governance rule 1

Ordinary reviews occur every three months and after a relevant change. Products, accounts, exceptions, certificates, and copies are checked. A deviation has an owner and date.

Governance rule 2

Revocation may affect a credential, policy, product, or purpose. The connector stops serving the product and retains a minimum record of the change. Consumers delete copies according to the agreement.

Governance rule 3

Incidents are communicated to affected organizations. The first action protects data and stops access. Closure records the cause, scope, actions, and preventive control.
