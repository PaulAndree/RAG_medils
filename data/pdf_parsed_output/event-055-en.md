First ARIADNA corpus review to inventory administrative documents, identify current and superseded versions, define the separation between development and evaluation, and agree on an initial freeze date.

Andrés Beltrán: ARIADNA will evaluate retrieval and generation over research-administration documentation. We need a clear snapshot of the corpus before creating questions.
Sergio Vidal: The initial inventory contains 184 Spanish documents and 172 English documents. Forty-six have more than one version.
Lucía Navarro: Parallel versions were not always published on the same day. We must record validity by language without assuming that a translation appeared simultaneously.
Tomás Leal: For evaluation, we must avoid treating an old and a new version as equivalent answers when deadlines or requirements have changed.
Pablo Herrero: The annotation tool can display the validity date and previous_version relation, but those fields are still missing from 31 documents.
Sergio Vidal: I propose three partitions: development with public documents and exploratory questions; validation for tuning retrieval; and a closed test set with documents and questions that will not be used during development.
Andrés Beltrán: Who will have access to the test set?
Sergio Vidal: Only me as custodian and one designated reviewer. Identifiers will be released after the experiments close.
Lucía Navarro: We must also balance Spanish and English by document type and difficulty.
Andrés Beltrán: Agreed. The first freeze will be on 20 February. Before that date, version relations, validity dates and bilingual equivalents will be completed.
