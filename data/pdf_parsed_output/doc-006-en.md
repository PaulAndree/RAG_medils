LUMEN COLLECTION WORK PLAN AND GOVERNANCE V1.0

1. Purpose

LUMEN develops a bilingual knowledge graph for collections held by the Regional Archive of the Centre. The project connects objects, people, places, dates, document types, events, and rights while retaining original description and uncertainty. It does not seek to replace archival systems or turn an interpretation into a definitive fact.

The plan covers source selection, terminology, modeling, ingestion, disambiguation, rights, the demonstrator, evaluation, and publication. Every activity must produce verifiable evidence. A coordination conversation does not itself alter a licence, model, or object selection; the decision must be recorded and approved by the competent party.

2. Principles

The Regional Archive validates meaning, context, and conditions of use. NEXUS designs transformations, software, tests, and documentation. Original data is retained alongside normalized values. A date such as “spring 1947” keeps its literal and receives an interpreted interval. A term without an exact equivalent retains its source term. An ambiguous person may remain an unlinked mention.

Every collection is classified as open, internal, restricted, or pending. The classification is retained in the manifest and controls which files, thumbnails, and metadata may reach the demonstrator and open subset. Technical availability never replaces authorization.

3. Governance

Lucía Navarro Gil leads the project. Eva Calderón Gil represents the Regional Archive. Yara Ben Salem leads bilingual terminology; Mateo Ruiz Serra reviews temporality, places, rights, and evaluation; Diego Ramos León works on entity linking; Sergio Vidal Cruz maintains the pipeline; Mireia Soler Pons reviews the model; Clara Ibáñez Soto coordinates licences, resources, and closure.

Decisions record an identifier, date, participants, issue, alternatives, rationale, affected artifacts, owner, and review. Approved, provisional, rejected, and revoked decisions are distinguished. A provisional decision may be used in an internal test but not in a release presented as stable.

Development section 1

The collection governance workstream has an owner, inputs, outputs, dependencies, and exit criteria. Execution begins from an identified source version and ends only where tests, a recorded review, and an explanation of exceptions exist. Changes are not made through isolated edits: they are added to the pipeline and retain provenance. The reviewer checks meaning, ES/EN equivalence, rights, and effects on other collections. If a change alters more records than expected, the run stops and is compared with the earlier version. Workstream 1 also maintains an open-issue register so that a provisional proposal cannot be treated as final.
