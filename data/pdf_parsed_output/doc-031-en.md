LUMEN DATA MANAGEMENT PLAN V1.0

1. Purpose

This plan defines how LUMEN receives, classifies, transforms, documents, stores, shares, publishes, retains, and deletes data from the Regional Archive of the Centre. It covers photographs, correspondence, posters, public notices, minute books, maps, inventories, authority records, and associated metadata.

The plan distinguishes digital files, descriptive metadata, transcriptions, vocabularies, linking tables, validation results, terminology decisions, and demonstrator products. Every category has a purpose, owner, location, access level, and retention period.

2. Principles

LUMEN follows minimization, traceability, security, quality, interoperability, version control, and respect for rights. Technical availability is not permission to use data. An old export is not presented as current. An interpreted value does not replace the original literal.

Data is classified as open, internal, restricted, or pending. This classification travels with the dataset and is enforced by the pipeline. Spanish and English pairs share identifiers and decisions even where their texts are naturally written in each language.
