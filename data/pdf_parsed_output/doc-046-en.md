ORION FINAL VALIDATION AND CLOSURE REPORT V1.0

1. Purpose

This report documents final validation of the reusable ORION package and the technical, documentary, and administrative closure of the project. Validation checks that research profiles, affiliations, projects, publications, datasets, software, and relations can be reused without losing provenance or confusing historical records with current states.

The final delivery contains normalized tables, an identity table, redirects, version relations, vocabularies, manifests, checksums, transformation rules, and exclusion documentation. Ambiguous records remain outside the reusable package and retain their exclusion reason.

2. Delivery status

The package contains 148 verified profiles, 312 affiliations, 96 projects, 1,284 publications, 41 datasets, and 73 software records. Eight profiles remain unresolved and stay in the pending inventory. Fifteen historical records are retained as earlier states and marked through supersession relations.

Final review identified zero duplicate identifiers, zero relations without a target, zero version cycles, and zero orphaned bilingual pairs. Tables were regenerated from a clean copy and compared with the reference export.

3. Closure principles

Closure does not remove project evidence. Decisions, scripts, validation results, and earlier versions are retained according to the archive plan. Provisional products are marked superseded. Temporary accounts and access are removed after documentation and required files have been transferred.

A resolved incident does not disappear from history. The report distinguishes the original defect, correction, closure test, and any residual limitation.
