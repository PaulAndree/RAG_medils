Eva noted that guide v1.0 uses creator, author, and producer almost as synonyms, which causes errors in archival descriptions. The working proposal distinguishes three functions:

- Creator: the person or organization responsible for creating, accumulating, or maintaining a body of records in its archival context.
- Author: the person responsible for the intellectual content of a specific work or document.
- Producer: the entity responsible for producing a physical or digital manifestation, such as a copy, edition, or digitization.

A resource may be linked to more than one function, but each relationship must be supported by the source. Author must not be inferred from creator. For photographic material, the photographer may be the author while an archive or studio acts as the creator of the collection.

The distinction is a working agreement, not final approval. Lucía will update the guide, and Eva will review examples before version 1.1 is published.
