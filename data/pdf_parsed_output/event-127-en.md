Second LUMEN demonstrator user evaluation to check accessibility and wording corrections, measure understanding of rights, dates and roles, and decide whether the version may be frozen for publication.

Lucía Navarro: Ten people are participating: six archive staff, two researchers and two students. We will repeat search and interpretation tasks and add rights cases.
Eva Calderón: Participants must identify whether an image may be reused, may only be viewed for education or is available as metadata only.
Mateo Ruiz: Nine of ten participants correctly interpreted all four states. One person confused metadata-only with an image that had not yet loaded.
Nuria Castaño: We will add an icon and the phrase 'reproduction unavailable because of conditions of use'.
Diego Ramos: For entity links, 92 percent distinguished confirmed from candidate. Errors were concentrated in color, not wording.
Yara Ben Salem: The bilingual definitions of author and records creator were better understood after shortening the text and adding examples.
Lucía Navarro: What was the result for uncertain dates?
Mateo Ruiz: Eighty-eight percent correctly interpreted circa dates, intervals and open bounds. Two people did not understand why an inferred date displayed an evidence source.
Eva Calderón: The source must remain, but we can change the label to 'basis of the estimate'.
Nuria Castaño: Keyboard and screen-reader accessibility passed every critical task.
Lucía Navarro: The candidate version is accepted, subject to three minor adjustments: a metadata-only icon, candidate-link color and the basis-of-estimate label. It may then be frozen.
