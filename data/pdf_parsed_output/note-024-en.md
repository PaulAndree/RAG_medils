Architecture v1.1 must replace the assumption of hourly data with native ingestion of 15-minute intervals. The connector will retain original readings inside SolCentro and generate aggregated products through an explicit, versioned, and auditable stage.

Three layers will be introduced: a restricted source zone, a processing zone for validation and aggregation, and a federated zone containing authorized products. Every product must record granularity, minimum meter count, aggregation rule, creation date, and input dataset version. Exports will not contain direct meter identifiers.

The version must also state that external testing is postponed until the security review is closed. Authentication will use the `verdant-pilot` audience, rotatable secrets, and revocable certificates. Document v1.0 will remain as the previous version to explain the change in assumptions.
